Frequently asked questions

A few helpful answers before you place your order.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart, enter your details, and send your order through WhatsApp. We will confirm availability and the final total with you.

Do you offer local delivery?

Yes, local delivery is available in selected areas. Delivery charges and availability are confirmed before we prepare your order.

Can I order a custom birthday or wedding cake?

Absolutely! Click “Enquire about custom cakes” to share your preferred flavours, size, theme and date through WhatsApp.

How much notice should I give?

For custom or large orders, advance notice is recommended. Please contact us to confirm how soon we can have your order ready.

How can I pay?

Payment options, including cash on delivery or online transfer where available, are confirmed over WhatsApp before an order is accepted.
